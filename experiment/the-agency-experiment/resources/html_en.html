2. Purpose of the research project
This research project aims to better understand how the brain of healthy adults works during a game where you have to beat your own scores and those of the computer.

3. Who can participate
You can participate in this study if you are 18 years old or older and in good health, with normal or corrected-to-normal vision, without a history of neurological or psychiatric disease, or known motor disorder.

4. What is expected of you
Your participation in this study consists of playing an online game. You will have to find an intruder among similar shapes and go click on it as fast as possible, in order to beat your own scores and those of the computer. You will also be asked to answer short questions about the experience, as well as a questionnaire at the end of the experience. The total duration of the study is estimated between 45 and 60 minutes.

5. Your rights to withdraw from research at any time
Your contribution to this research is voluntary and you must give your consent before participating. You are free to stop participating at any time. Your decision to participate, refuse to participate, or stop participating can be made at any time without justification and will have no effect on you. If you do not wish to participate, or if you wish to stop participating, you can simply close the browser window at any time. If you leave the game after starting it, your previously collected data will be saved.

6. Your rights to confidentiality and privacy
All data collected is completely anonymous and at no time it is possible to identify you. Furthermore, you will not be asked for any information that could reveal your identity.

7. Data retention
The data from this study will be kept for 5 years on the server of the Lyon Neuroscience Research Center, then archived on this same server for a period of 8 years before being destroyed. They may also be deposited on open access platforms.

8. Ethical aspects
This project received a favorable opinion from the Inserm Ethics Evaluation Committee (IRB00003888) on June 7th 2022.

9. Dissemination
The results of the study will be disseminated in scientific congresses, scientific journals and may be the subject of teaching and appear in educational materials.

10. Your rights to ask questions at any time
You can contact the scientific manager of this research at any time to request further information on the study (marine . vernet (at) inserm . fr).
